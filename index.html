EdgeORE

Wallet-signed ORE deploys with on-device receipts.

EdgeORE is a non-custodial Android dapp for Solana Mobile (package com.edgeore.app). Connect your wallet with Mobile Wallet Adapter, check your SOL, ORE and SKR balances, and use the ORE v3 grid. Before any transaction EdgeORE shows the exact message for your review; only your wallet signs it, and EdgeORE submits it once. After each action EdgeORE saves a receipt signed with Android Keystore, and its receipt checker verifies the integrity of signed receipt contents.

Mainnet deploys spend real SOL, which may be lost. Each deploy is capped at 0.01 SOL and needs you to confirm the risk. EdgeORE does not promise any reward or income. Use devnet to try it with no real funds. This is a hackathon build (0.1.0-clockin); features may change.
