Troubleshooting

Problem What to do
INSTALL_FAILED_UPDATE_INCOMPATIBLE An EdgeORE signed with another certificate is installed. Export anything you need, then uninstall it (this deletes its on-device data).
Wallet screen blank or no accounts Make sure your wallet supports the network chosen in the selector, then tap Connect again.
Balances show Not read No wallet is connected or the RPC read failed. Connect your wallet on the selected network and check your internet connection.
Review refuses The reason is shown right under the button. Fix what it names, then review again.
"Round changed" ORE rounds last about a minute. Start a fresh review; EdgeORE never resends a signed transaction.
Outcome unknown EdgeORE checks chain state after a restart and never resends. The operation stays visible until the chain reports it.
Transfers disabled The operation store is damaged or a write failed. Restart EdgeORE; it will not treat a damaged store as empty.
