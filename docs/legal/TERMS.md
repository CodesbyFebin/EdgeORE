## 1. What EdgeORE is
EdgeORE is a non-custodial Android client for the ORE v3 program on Solana. It shows public ORE board data and helps you build ORE deploys and devnet SOL transfers. You review each one before your own wallet signs it. EdgeORE never holds your private keys or your funds, and cannot move funds without your signature.
