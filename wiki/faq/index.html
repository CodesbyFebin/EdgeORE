FAQ

Does EdgeORE hold my keys? No. Keys stay in your MWA wallet; EdgeORE receives only your public address and the transactions you approve.

Can I earn with it? EdgeORE does not promise, guarantee or estimate any reward or income. ORE outcomes are decided by the ORE protocol.

What does a mainnet deploy cost? At most 0.01 SOL per deploy plus network fees; a first deploy also pays miner-account rent. The SOL may be lost.

Can I test without real SOL? Yes, use Devnet.

Is an AI model bundled? No. Use an owned host or download an allowlisted on-device model after consent.

Does it make ZK proofs? Not in this build: "Prover not configured".

Is it affiliated with ORE or Solana Mobile? No. It is independent; upstream attribution is in NOTICE.
