Wiki

EdgeORE is a non-custodial Android dapp for Solana Mobile (com.edgeore.app) by CodesbyFebin. It shows the exact message before any transaction, has your Mobile Wallet Adapter wallet sign it, submits it once, and saves a receipt signed with Android Keystore.

  • Source: https://github.com/CodesbyFebin/EdgeORE
  • Releases: https://github.com/CodesbyFebin/EdgeORE/releases
  • User docs: codesbyfebin.github.io/EdgeORE/docs

EdgeORE is a hackathon build (0.1.0-clockin). It does not promise rewards or income, and mainnet deploys spend real SOL (capped at 0.01 SOL per deploy).
