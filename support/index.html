Support

Questions and bugs

Open an issue on GitHub Issues. Include your Android version, wallet app and the EdgeORE build shown in Settings.

Security issues

Report privately as described in SECURITY.md, not in a public issue.

Email

support@REPLACE-WITH-CONTACT-EMAIL (placeholder until a support address is published).

EdgeORE will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Transactions are signed only in your own wallet and cannot be reversed.
